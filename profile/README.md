# Peysaz

**Building-materials supply, run on a platform we build ourselves.**

Peysaz supplies cement and construction safety equipment from leading factories, to wholesale and
retail buyers. Everything behind it (ordering, stock, delivery, invoicing, payments and customer
messaging) runs on a platform we design and build in-house.

---

### 🏗️ For businesses

Buy cement and safety equipment, wholesale or retail, at **[peysaz.com](https://peysaz.com)**.

### 🤝 For partners

Suppliers, logistics and payment providers: our platform connects to the outside world through an
API. Our own storefront is built entirely on it. If you want to work with us, reach out through
**[peysaz.com](https://peysaz.com)**.

### 🧩 For developers

We are opening the platform for others to build on. It isn't open yet. Follow this organization to
hear when it is.

### What's new: 5.0.0 (2026-11-11)

- A home page for each role: drivers see their load and the cash to collect, customers see their open order, delivery and balance, and staff see what is waiting on them today.
- Order again in one tap: your most frequent products, with the usual quantity filled in.
- Set any account to active, suspended or blocked from one control, with every block and its reason kept on record. Integrations that sign people in must handle the new blocked state.
- Send price announcements by how active your customers are, reach people who joined recently, and see exactly who receives each message and why.
- Choose at which order steps your customer invoices are issued.
- Choose how many rows each main list shows.
